        Gini or entropy? either one
        
Same shape, different scale. Gini tops out at 0.5, entropy at 1 bit. On these
          twelve students they pick the same question at every node.

        
What matters is which way it moves. A good question splits a group into
          groups with lower impurity. How much lower is the information gain, in tab 03.

        
Some books, and scikit-learn's log_loss option, call this
          entropy cross-entropy. At a tree node it is the same number.

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        03
        
Information gain: how much one question lowers the impurity

        

          Each column is one question. The dashed line is the impurity before asking, the
          blue bar is what is left after, and the green gap is the gain. The biggest gap
          wins. Switch the ruler: the numbers change, the winner does not.
        

      


      

        gain  =  I(before)  −  I(after)
        
          I(after) =
          nyesn·I(yes side)
           + 
          nnon·I(no side)
        
        
          I = Gini   1 − Σp²
          I = entropy (cross-entropy)   −Σp·log₂p
        
        
          I is the "information" in information gain. Pick either ruler; the rule stays
          the same: the bigger the drop, the higher the gain. The weights let the bigger side
          count for more.
